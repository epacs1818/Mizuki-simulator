1. WHAT THIS TOOL DOES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The calculator simulates a full party rotation over time and reports what
each hit deals, the total damage and the overall DPS. It models every
character, weapon and artifact set shipped with this version, including
their triggered effects (extra hits, buff windows, stack decay, cooldowns
and reaction damage).

The simulation is event based: each ability schedules its hits at fixed
offsets from the cast, the timeline resolves them in order, and the buffs
of every hit are recomputed from the state at that moment. Because of that,
changing a rotation's timings can change the damage of every later hit.
On cast (or on button press) effects are considered before the first hit of the action.

The abilities hit timings are based on my setup's performance including fps and ping.
All the timeline data is taken from personal tests on the training dummy.
The available preset trials take into account my personal timinings as well.

All the calculations made are presented as transparently as possible via the timeline table.
Any mistakes found can be sent to me, I'll gladly try to solve them.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THIS TOOL DOES NOT DO
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The calculator does not calculate particle generation and energy recharge
requirements for the party.
It does not calculate events based on the distance of the enemy.
It does not consider the frames of the attacks. It's just a simulation of scheduled hits.
It does not calculate aoe damage.
It does not simulate a random trial. All damage values are averages (unless everyone has 100% cr).

3. Quick start
  1.  Fill the four party slots in Tab 1 and press Confirm party configuration.
      Artifacts are optional. After you confirm your party, you can check their off field stats.
  2.  Set the enemy level and resistances in Tab 2 and confirm. Check the enemy card
  3.  Pick a trial in Tab 3 and press Run Trial Simulation.
  4.  Read the results in Tab 4.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TAB 1 - TEAM & GEAR SETUP
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Four slots are shown. For each slot choose:

  Character       The character for that slot.
                  Mizuki/Odette/Anastasya/Aether/Faruzan/Sucrose/Diona
  Level           60 / 70 / 80 / 90 / 95 / 100.
                  A4/A4/A5/A6/A7/A8 respectively.
                  A4 minimum to guarantee all characters' passives.
  Constellation   C0/C1/C2/C3/C4/C5/C6.
                  C3 and C5 are currently decorative only.
                  Unavailable constellations are not implemented in the program.
  Weapon          Only weapons that character can equip are listed.
  Refinement      R1/R2/R3/R4/R5.
  Talent Levels   Normal / Skill / Burst, e.g. 1/13/13.
                  Includes C3 and C5 bonuses.

Artifact set and pieces
  Choose the set, then set each piece's main stat, rarity and up to four
  substats. Flower and Feather have fixed main stats.

Substat standard (carries only)
  Manual                    You enter mains and substats yourself.
  100% RV (Crit Value)      The best-case crit-value distribution for that
                            character; mains and substats are generated.
  KQM Standards (40 rolls)  A more forgiving 40-roll standard. https://compendium.keqingmains.com/

  The two standards lock the main stats AND the substats, and model crit as
  one Crit Value line. They are only offered for the characters that have a
  standard defined.

  Currently ER requirements are not taken into consideration in neither 100% RV or KQM Standards mode.

Press CONFIRM PARTY CONFIGURATION when the four slots are ready. The stat
cards appear under the section header and stay visible while you edit the
slots and artifacts below.

If you change gear, substats or the substat standard after confirming, the
app warns you and hides the cards until you confirm again. Running a trial
always uses the current inputs, confirmed or not.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TAB 2 - ENEMY CONFIGURATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Set the enemy level and its resistance for each element (Pyro, Cryo, Hydro,
Electro, Anemo, Geo, Dendro and Physical), then press CONFIRM ENEMY
CONFIGURATION. Resistance shred from buffs and artifacts is applied on top
of these values when damage is calculated.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TAB 3 - ROTATION SETUP
----------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Running a trial
  Pick a trial from the dropdown. Preset trials only appear when the party
  is exactly the team they were written for; custom trials always appear.
  The TRIAL BREAKDOWN expander shows the description, the end mode, the
  trial duration, and every rotation with its field time and steps:

      Rotation #1: Standard Mizuki-Faruzan Loop [19.702s] (Repeated 6x)
        Step 1: Aether EN1 (Aether - 1.23s field time)
        Step 2: Faruzan Q (Faruzan - 1.41s field time)
        ...

  END MODE
    Time       The trial stops at the duration you set.
    Last swap  The trial ends when the last scheduled character swaps out.
               The duration shown is the sum of every field time.

  Press RUN TRIAL SIMULATION. Each run is stored in the history of Tab 4.
  If a rotation asks for an ability before its cooldown is over, the run is
  stopped with a Cooldown Error instead of producing results.

5.2 Field sequences
  A field sequence is one character's visit: which abilities they use, when
  each one starts (seconds after the swap-in) and how long they stay on
  field. Build one in "Create Custom Field Sequence":
    - enter the name, the character and the field (swap-out) time;
    - pick an ability and its insertion time, then press "Add";
    - repeat for every ability of the visit, then Save Field Sequence.

  Only abilities of characters in the party are offered. Engine-injected
  procs (extra hits, triggered abilities) can never be added by hand: they
  fire on their own conditions.

5.3 Rotations
  A rotation is an ordered list of field sequences plus a repetition count.
  In "Create Custom Rotation" add sequences one by one. The same sequence
  can be added more than once, which is useful for patterns such as "burst
  every other loop": put both variants in one rotation and repeat it.
  Use the arrows to reorder and the X to remove an entry, then Save
  Rotation.

5.4 Trials
  A trial combines rotations and defines when it ends. In the trial
  assembly area set the name and description, choose the end mode (Time or
  Last swap) and, for Time, the duration. Add rotations with their
  repetitions using "Add Rotation to Trial", then press Create Trial.

  "Load selected trial into editor" copies the selected trial's rotations
  into the editor so they can be tweaked and re-used, including the shipped
  presets.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TAB 4 - SIMULATION RESULTS & HISTORY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Each run is stored with the party, gear, enemy, trial and custom setups it
used. The newest run is expanded first. An entry can be renamed, deleted
and exported as a build file.

  Total Damage    Sum of every hit's average damage in the trial.
  Overall DPS     Total Damage divided by the trial duration.
  Trial Duration  The duration used for the run: the configured window for
                  a Time trial, the sum of the field times for Last swap.

  Charts show the damage by element, by hit type and by character
  (reactions excluded), plus a per-second damage bar chart.

  ROTATION TIMELINE TABLE
    One row per event: time, active character, owner, action, element
    application, aura, reaction and damage. Tick "Show detailed columns"
    for the full audit view (buff states, stacks, multipliers, ...).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7. SAVING AND LOADING A BUILD
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Team & Gear Setup -> Save / Load Build

  Export current build (.json)
      Writes the party, gear (mains and substats), enemy, custom field
      sequences, rotations and trials, and the selected trial.

  Upload a previously saved build JSON file
      Restores the file and keeps every input editable. A file is applied
      only once; upload it again to start over from it.

  User manual (.txt)
      Downloads this file.

The same export is available per run in Tab 4 ("Export Run"), carrying the
run's own party, gear and trial.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
8. TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
"Please configure and create your party first..."
    Fill all four slots in Tab 1 and press Confirm party configuration.

"Please confirm your enemy configuration first..."
    Press Confirm enemy configuration in Tab 2.

"Gear, substats or the substat standard changed..."
    The confirmed party no longer matches the inputs. Press Confirm party
    configuration to rebuild it.

"No trial is available for this team."
    A preset trial is tied to one specific team. Build a custom trial for
    any other party.

"Cooldown Error! <character>'s '<ability>' is on CD until <t>s..."
    The rotation asks for an ability before its cooldown ends. Move the cast
    later or remove the extra cast, then run again.

"Field sequence '...': ability '...' no longer exists..."
    The imported build referenced an ability that is not part of this
    version; that call was skipped.

A trial ends with little or no damage:
    Check the Trial Breakdown. A Time trial can end before the first cast if
    the duration is shorter than the rotation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
9. ASSUMPTIONS AND LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Rotations are idealized: abilities start exactly at the times you enter
  and every hit resolves at its fixed offset from the cast.
- Hits keep resolving after a character swaps out (for example a summon
  that stays on field). Nothing is cancelled by a swap.
- Energy and particles are not simulated. Energy Recharge is only a stat.
- Constellation levels that only raise talent levels (for example C3 and C5)
  are not applied automatically; enter the final talent levels yourself.
- The enemy is a single target with fixed resistances and the damage model
  of this version.
- Damage is reported as an average (crit rate x crit damage expected value).
