notes about how stellar swirl and vortex work

Faruzan
VV/FaruzanBuff/FaruzanDebuff gets applied to the swirl of the attack that casted it.
Faruzan Buff only affects active characters.

Faruzan test. 100 EM (0.2857 em_bonus). Base damage at lv 90 = 1446.853458. Stellar swirl reduces base damage to 75% of its value. So, 0.75*1446.853458=1085.140094.

97% CD + 40% from FaruzanBuff makes crit_mult (100%cr) =  2.37. No elevation (mult = 1).
Enemy anemo res = 10%, with 30% shred from FaruzanDebuff, res_mult = 1.1.
Total damage = 5085.6.
Only contributor (60% share) = 3051.4

How would Mizuki's c1 quill affect the anemo stelar reaction damage with contributions from both aether and Mizuki?
It's applied after the share. So, in this example it would calculate the base damage of the quill = 5.50*1298 = 7139. Skip bonuses like mizuki's skill share, stellar_bonus, em_bonus. Multiply by Mizuki's crit_mult = 2.92 and res_mult=1.05. This will give a quill of 21888.174
that gets added to the previous combined anemo stellar swirl of 13422. So the total damage of the swirl is 13422 + 21888 = 35310.6.
In average calculaations, when the crit_rate isn't maxed out the anemo_swirl expected value is a function of the 2^4 cases (since each contributor can crit or not and the order of contribution can change based on that),
the swirl damage is statistical, but the quill is always constant so it should be added after the expected value calculation.


Important: the cryo stellar vortex explosion calculates the damage based on the contributor's stats AT THE TIME OF THE EXPLOSION AND NOT SNAPSHOTTED AT THE CONTRIBUTION.
Same for cryo characters' contribution in the anemo stellar swirls, the apply or refresh aura gets the credit but its damage will only be calculated at the time of the swirl.
Faruzan buff gives 40% anemo crit damage. So if it's active in the character their swirl will use this buff, EVEN FOR CRYO CHARACTERS because cryo characters can still
deal anemo damage via swirl contribution. That's noticed if a swirl happens when a cryo contributor is on field (because Faruzan only buffs onfielders, see first line of this file),
the swirl will consider an addition 40% crit damage from this cryo character.

While Aether's C2 enhanced doesn't affect the stellar damage that activated it, it will affect the stellar swirl that follows the attack that activated it via stellar swirl.
